ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK 2024
=================================================

SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
--------------------------------
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.
Leave must be approved by the direct manager at least 2 weeks in advance.
Unused leave can be carried over for a maximum of 5 days into the following year.
Any leave exceeding the carry-over limit will be forfeited.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY
--------------------------------
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the employee's local timezone.
Employees must be available on company communication channels during core hours.
Remote work equipment (laptop, headset) is provided by the company.
Home internet costs are NOT reimbursed by the company.

SECTION 3: HEALTH AND WELLNESS BENEFITS
-----------------------------------------
The company provides a health insurance allowance of $500 per month per employee.
Employees are eligible for a gym membership subsidy of $50 per month.
Mental health support includes 6 free counselling sessions per year through the EAP program.
Vision care: employees receive $200 annual allowance for glasses or contact lenses.
Dental care: employees receive $300 annual allowance.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE REVIEW CYCLE
--------------------------------------
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: in June and December.
Employees receive written feedback within 2 weeks of the review.
Salary adjustments, if any, take effect from the first of the following month.
Promotions require approval from both the direct manager and HR.

SECTION 5: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
----------------------------------
Business travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of travel.
Maximum meal allowance for domestic travel: $75 per day.
Maximum meal allowance for international travel: $150 per day.
Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.
Expense reports must be submitted via the company expense portal (expenses.acme.com).

SECTION 6: CODE OF CONDUCT
----------------------------
All employees must maintain professional standards in all communications.
Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated.
Conflicts of interest must be declared to HR in writing within 5 business days of arising.
All company data and intellectual property must be kept confidential.
Violations of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

SECTION 7: EQUIPMENT AND SECURITY
------------------------------------
Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.
Unauthorized software installation on company devices is prohibited.
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security within 4 hours.

SECTION 8: PARENTAL LEAVE
---------------------------
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Parental leave can commence up to 4 weeks before the expected birth/adoption date.
Employees must give at least 8 weeks notice of planned parental leave.
