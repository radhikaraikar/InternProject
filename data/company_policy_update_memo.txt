ACME CORPORATION — INTERNAL MEMO
DATE: March 15, 2024
FROM: HR Director, Sarah Mitchell
TO: All Employees
RE: Policy Updates — Effective April 1, 2024

Dear Team,

Please note the following UPDATES to our Employee Policy Handbook. These supersede 
previous policy where there is a conflict.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE 1: ANNUAL LEAVE — REVISED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Following the annual review, annual leave entitlement for full-time employees 
has been INCREASED to 25 days per calendar year (up from 20 days).
Carry-over limit remains at 5 days.

NOTE: The previous policy document stated 20 days — this has been updated 
to 25 days as of April 2024.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE 2: REMOTE WORK — REVISED  
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The remote work policy has been updated. Employees may now work remotely 
up to 4 days per week (increased from 3 days), effective April 1, 2024.
Manager approval is still required.
Core hours remain unchanged at 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE 3: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT — REVISED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The meal allowance for international travel has been updated to $175 per day 
(previously $150 per day) to reflect rising costs.
Domestic meal allowance remains at $75 per day.
The receipt threshold is now $20 (previously $25).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE 4: HOME INTERNET REIMBURSEMENT — NEW POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Effective April 1, 2024, the company will reimburse employees up to $50 per 
month for home internet costs when working remotely. This is a NEW benefit.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
UPDATE 5: PARENTAL LEAVE — NO CHANGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Parental leave policy remains unchanged (16 weeks primary / 4 weeks secondary).

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Please review these updates carefully. The full revised handbook will be 
published to the intranet by April 1, 2024.

For questions, contact hr@acme.com.

Sarah Mitchell
HR Director, ACME Corporation
